# Frameworks & Mental Models

Mental models, frameworks, and named concepts extracted from the Codex Vitae and personal reading. Alphabetized with one-line descriptions and source attribution.

---

## A

**Antifragility** — Systems that gain strength from disorder and volatility, rather than merely surviving it. Opposite of fragile; more than robust. *(Nassim Nicholas Taleb, Antifragile)*

## B

**Belief Categorization (4 Types)** — A framework for organizing beliefs: (1) Metabeliefs: beliefs about beliefs, (2) Perceptions: beliefs about how reality seems to be, (3) Opinions: beliefs about meaning and how to approach life, (4) Predictions: beliefs about the future. *(Codex Vitae)*

## C

**Codex Vitae** — A personal "Book of Life" capturing everything learned — beliefs, models, principles — so that if you lost your memories, it could reconstruct who you are. A living document updated over time. *(Robin Sloan, Mr Penumbra's 24-Hour Bookstore; personal practice since 2012)*

**Compound Interest (beliefs)** — The benefits of maintaining a belief document grow in a compound interest kind of way: beliefs course-correct over time and build on one another. *(Codex Vitae)*

**Consciousness as Story** — Consciousness receives a stream of observer moments and turns them into stories. Each observer moment is a static freeze-frame of the universe, updated each moment with a fading memory of past moments. *(Codex Vitae, 2A)*

## D

**Death Bed Game** — A heuristic for evaluating choices: will this matter on your death bed? Frequently remembering you will die increases the quality of your life. *(Codex Vitae; "The Death Bed Game" essay)*

## H

**Hume's Fork** — Two kinds of knowledge exist: (1) matters of fact (never knowable with certainty, only not-yet-disproven) and (2) relations of ideas (self-referencing network of concepts). What we call "truth" is merely an idea that fits into the network without contradiction. *(David Hume; Codex Vitae)*

## M

**Mental Heuristics & Bias Accumulation** — We create mental heuristics to reduce energy in retrieving and selecting from possibilities. The byproduct: we are biased at all times in proportion to the number of heuristics we use. We are the sum of our biases. *(Codex Vitae, 2A)*

**Monoculture Risk** — Systems that converge on a single design become predictable and efficient but brittle when environments change. Optionality is required even in stable environments. *(Codex Vitae; The Fifth Discipline)*

## O

**Observer Moments** — Each moment of experience is a static model of the universe captured in freeze frame, with a frozen model of past, present, future, and every concept, updated anew each moment. *(Codex Vitae, 2A)*

**Optionality** — Maintaining multiple paths and possibilities rather than converging on one design. Closely related to antifragility. The quiet, underrated power of keeping options open. *(Nassim Nicholas Taleb; Codex Vitae)*

## P

**Perception = Change Detection** — Perception and sensation are the awareness of things changing. We cannot perceive or sense things that aren't changing. *(Codex Vitae, 2A)*

**Primes** — Ideas recently activated in our brains that color the lenses through which we experience everything else. Related to soloverse, biases, identity, and quality time. *(Codex Vitae)*

## Q

**Quality Time (3 Types)** — Three kinds of quality time worth seeking: (1) with yourself — finding your core interests and self-knowledge, (2) with your interests — deepening ideas and skills, (3) with others — connecting deeply with people. Each feeds the others. *(Codex Vitae)*

**Quantum Realism** — The assertion that the physical world isn't real in itself but is the output or projection of a quantum reality generating it — essentially a virtual reality or simulation. Opposed to "physical realism." *(Brian Whitworth; Codex Vitae)*

## R

**Rational Emotive Behavior Therapy (REBT)** — A framework for understanding that beliefs (not events) cause emotional disturbance, and that irrational beliefs can be actively disputed and replaced. *(Albert Ellis; personal canon)*

## S

**Simulation Hypothesis** — We probably live in a simulation. The physical world is an output of a deeper computational or quantum reality. Related to quantum realism. *(Codex Vitae, 2C)*

**Soloverse** — Your mental model of the universe that your brain uses to think about the universe. You can't think about the universe directly — you can only think about your soloverse. Related to the German concept *umwelt* (an organism's subjective world). *(Codex Vitae; coined personally)*

**Stock and Flow** — Two fundamental types of content/value: stock (durable, accumulated value that compounds) and flow (real-time output that generates attention). Strong long-term strategy requires both. *(Robin Sloan, "Stock and Flow")*

**Systems Thinking** — A way of understanding how resources, feedback loops, and relationships cycle through various incarnations in a system over time. Explains how changing one part of a system affects the whole. *(Peter Senge, The Fifth Discipline; Codex Vitae)*

## U

**Umwelt** — German for "environment/surroundings." Each organism has a subjective world defined by what it can perceive and interact with. Humans each have their own umwelt; the soloverse is a personal umwelt for one's mental model of reality. *(Jakob von Uexküll; Codex Vitae)*
