## Behavior Change

- **Ridiculous energy required** — Changing behavior and habits requires a ridiculous amount of energy. Most of the time we don't change ourselves; we are changed by other things. *(Codex Vitae, 2A)*
- **Stories enable change** — The best way for brains to model predictions is to create stories; behavior change is most sustainable when framed narratively. *(Codex Vitae, 2A)*

## Beliefs & Epistemology

- **4 belief types** — Beliefs can be sorted into 4 categories: metabeliefs, perceptions, opinions, predictions. This categorization reduces internal contradiction. *(Codex Vitae)*
- **Compound growth of self-knowledge** — Benefits of a belief document grow compound-interest-style over time. *(Codex Vitae)*

## Cognition & Bias

- **Bias is proportional to heuristics** — We are biased at all times in proportion to the number of mental heuristics we use. More shortcuts = more bias. *(Codex Vitae, 2A)*
- **We favor beautiful, reaffirming, expected** — Documented cognitive tendencies: we favor the beautiful, the reaffirming, and the expected; we over-credit success and under-credit luck. *(Codex Vitae, 2A)*
- **Models ≠ reality** — We all build an incomplete model of the universe and operate under the assumption it is real. It's practical but not accurate. *(Codex Vitae, 2A)*

## Technology & Future

- **Self-driving displacement by 2025** — Self-driving vehicles will begin replacing truck drivers, impacting employment rates. *(Codex Vitae, Prediction)*
- **Computers building computers by 2050** — Computers will be building better computers than humans by 2050. *(Codex Vitae, Prediction)*
- **Basic income in majority of first-world countries by 2050** — Employment will be decoupled from having the means to live. *(Codex Vitae, Prediction)*
- **Solar overtakes fossil fuels by 2050** — Solar will overcome gas and oil in usage. *(Codex Vitae, Prediction)*
- **Privacy traded for connection by 2050** — People, organizations, and governments will exchange almost all privacy of personal data for interpersonal connection and ML personalization. It will be the norm. *(Codex Vitae, Prediction)*
- **Virtual reality replaces physical travel by 2100** — Physical travel will be completely unnecessary due to VR technology. *(Codex Vitae, Prediction)*
- **Super organism by 2200** — Humans, computers, and Earth will evolve into at least one super organism or networked brain. *(Codex Vitae, Prediction)*

## Genomics & Biology

- **CRISPR editing by 2025** — Technology to program DNA via CRISPR will allow easy, cheap editing, removal, and addition of DNA in embryos. First applications: disease prevention (e.g., Alzheimer's). *(Codex Vitae, Prediction)*
- **GMO foods normalized by 2030** — Safe, genetically modified foods will become the norm. *(Codex Vitae, Prediction)*

## Education

- **College-equivalent online by 2028** — The equivalent of a college education in breadth of knowledge and career value will be available to anyone with an internet connection. *(Codex Vitae, Prediction — wager with Carinna)*

## Quality of Life

- **3 types of quality time** — Quality time comes in three forms: with yourself, with your interests, with others. Each feeds the others. *(Codex Vitae)*
- **Death awareness improves life** — Frequently remembering we will die increases the quality of our life. *(Codex Vitae, 2B)*
